## 01 / Understand the pattern {color="green"}
### 1. Three questions for every consonant
**Where** does the air stop? (lips, tongue tip, back of tongue)
**How** does it move? (a full stop, a hiss, through the nose...)
**Is your voice on?** Put your fingers on your throat and say *sss → zzz*. The buzz you feel on /z/ is voicing.
Today's six sounds are **stops**: the air is blocked completely for a moment, and then released.

### 2. English has two stops where Korean has three
Korean has three kinds of stops at each place (ㅂ/ㅃ/ㅍ, ㄷ/ㄸ/ㅌ, ㄱ/ㄲ/ㅋ). English has only two (/p/–/b/). The good news: your Korean sounds are a useful starting point.
<table fit-page-width="true" header-row="true">
<tr>
<td>English</td>
<td>Closest Korean</td>
<td>🇰🇷 Tip</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>/p t k/ at the start of a stressed syllable</td>
<td>ㅍ ㅌ ㅋ</td>
<td>A strong puff of air, just like ㅍ ㅌ ㅋ. Hold a tissue in front of your mouth: it should move.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>/b d ɡ/</td>
<td>ㅂ ㄷ ㄱ</td>
<td>Little or no puff. Start your voice early, as if you hum a tiny "m" first: (m)bag. Never use ㅃ ㄸ ㄲ.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>/p t k/ **after /s/** (sp, st, sk)</td>
<td>ㅃ ㄸ ㄲ</td>
<td>**No puff.** *spin* ≈ s+삔, *stop* ≈ s+땁, *sky* ≈ s+까이. Korean spelling (스핀, 스톱, 스카이) adds a puff **and** an extra 으 beat.</td>
</tr>
</table>

### 3. Stops at the end of a word
**The s+ㄲ trick:** Say 까이 (tense, no air). Now put a hissing *s* in front with no vowel: *s-까이* → *sky*. One beat. Do the same for *s-땁* → *stop* and *s-꿀* → *school*.
In Korean, a final ㄱ, ㄷ, or ㅂ is held but not released (박, 밭, 밥). English final stops are also often held quietly, so this part is easy. The danger is **adding 으 to release them**: *bed* → 베드, *robe* → 로브. That adds a whole extra beat.
English uses another clue to tell *bet* and *bed* apart: **the vowel before a voiced stop (b, d, g) is longer**.

<callout icon="🇰🇷" color="yellow_bg">
	**Two traps with final stops:**
	1. **Adding 으:** 베드, 로브, 백그 → each gains a beat. Keep the word to **one beat (●)**. Close your mouth for the final sound and stop.
	2. **Merging:** 백 for both *back* and *bag*. Let the vowel in *bag* take more time: *ba—g*.
</callout>
