## Start here
Reading here means **reading aloud with pronunciation and meaning**. <br>We use common American English patterns as a learning model. <br>Different accents are normal; the goal is a clear message.
**The big idea:** Korean gives every syllable an equal beat (바-나-나). English gives each word **one strong beat** and squeezes the rest (ba-**NA**-na). <br>It also packs up to three consonants into a single beat (*strike* = 1 beat, 스트라이크 = 5). Every lesson comes back to this.
Work through the lessons in order. Each combines related topics, so you can compare several sounds or patterns on one page. Use one session for the explanation and word groups, then another for the sentence sets and short reading. Adjust the pace to your needs.
## Your lesson routine
1. **Understand:** read the short explanation and today's goal.
2. **Compare:** read the word tables aloud. Say Example A, then B, then A again, and tap the beat pattern. The 🇰🇷 columns show where Korean spelling hides a difference.
3. **Keep the 박자:** do the rhythm drill. Clap at a steady speed and fit every line onto the same claps.
4. **Read aloud:** the warm-up, the PRACTICE sets, and a short passage. Every sentence is shown as **plain text first**. Decide the stress yourself, then open the ▶ toggle under it to check the marked answer.
5. **Check:** answer the questions, open the answer key, and tick the "Before the next lesson, I can…" checklist.
<callout icon="🗝️" color="gray_bg">
	**Reading marks used in every lesson**
	**●** strong beat (longer, louder, higher, clear vowel) · **·** weak beat (short, quiet, relaxed vowel)
	**CAPITALS** = stressed syllable: ba-NA-na. Only a word or syllable written **fully** in capitals is stressed.
	**BOLD CAPITALS** = focus word (the main pitch move) · **↘** fall · **↗** rise
	**\|** = small pause · **‿** = link the sounds together (like 연음)
	**🇰🇷 yellow boxes** = tips and traps for Korean speakers
	**How to tap:** hit the desk hard on ●, lightly on ·.
	These marks are practice guides, not the only correct reading.
</callout>
